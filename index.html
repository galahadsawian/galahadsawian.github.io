The Canvas of the Clouds

Matthew S Dkhar Sawian | 24bjmc20

While the roaring amplifiers gave Shillong its title, the city's true creative pulse extends far beyond sound. The streets are a living runway; the walls are an open-air gallery.

The Street Style Runway

Image Archive // Street Style + Winter Lookbook

Walk down Police Bazar on any given evening, and you will see a masterclass in styling. Fashion in Shillong isn't dictated by passing online trends; it is a carefully curated culture built on individuality, climate necessity, and a deep appreciation for thrift culture.

The aesthetic is heavily influenced by the music. Vintage leather jackets, heavy boots, and band tees are seamlessly blended with traditional Khasi Ryndia silk scarves. The youth have perfected the art of layering, mixing high-street global fashion with rare thrift finds. It is visual communication projecting a bold, alternative identity.

The Open-Air Gallery

Artwork Profiles // Muralists Collective + Abstract Expressions

If fashion is what the people wear, art is what the city wears. The blank walls and retaining structures of Shillong's winding roads have been transformed into massive canvases. These visual projects tell stories of indigenous Khasi folklore, environmental conservation, and the intersection of modern youth culture with ancestral roots.

Beyond street art, the contemporary gallery scene is thriving. Local artists experiment heavily with mixed media, utilizing raw local materials alongside digital platforms. During events, large-scale immersive art installations take over, turning the landscape into a canvas of light, nature, and shadow.
