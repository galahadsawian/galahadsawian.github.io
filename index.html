The Music Capital of India

Written by Matthew S Dkhar Sawian
Media Manager & Visual Communications Producer

Every time I book a WizzRide shuttle from Guwahati and wind my way up into the East Khasi Hills, the shift in the atmosphere is palpable. The clouds descend, the air cools, and you can practically hear a guitar riff echoing through the pines. Having produced media campaigns and music videos, I've spent hours analyzing what makes a live performance connect with an audience. But you don't need a producer's eye to see what's happening in Meghalaya. You just have to listen.

"The music in Shillong is not background. It is infrastructure... A city with this much active music culture does not happen by accident. Shillong happened because of intention." [1]

Shillong isn’t just a hill station; it is a global stage. The sheer gravity of the music scene here pulls artists from every corner of the planet. Whether you are hopping in a Rapido to catch a local jam session in a neighborhood cafe, or standing shoulder-to-shoulder with thousands at the Cherry Blossom Festival, live music is the undisputed heartbeat of this city.

20 Artists Who Defined the Shillong Sound

You can't claim the title of the Music Capital without the roster to back it up. Here are 20 artists across three tiers who have left a permanent mark on the Khasi Hills.

The Hometown Heroes (5)

  • Soulmate: Asia’s premier blues-rock export, born and raised right here.
  • Lou Majaw: The undisputed legend, keeping Bob Dylan's spirit alive on the streets.
  • Snow White: Perfecting the fusion of heavy rock with traditional Khasi lyricism.
  • Khasi Bloodz: The crew that established Shillong as a serious hip-hop stronghold.
  • Summersalt: Masters of blending folk instrumentation with modern cinematic arrangements.

The Indian Heavyweights (5)

  • Papon: A massive crowd-puller bridging regional folk and contemporary anthems.
  • Nucleya: Bringing ground-shaking bass music to the high altitudes.
  • Indian Ocean: Delivering deeply spiritual fusion-rock sets as the sun sets over the hills.
  • Amit Trivedi: Turning the polo grounds into a massive Bollywood-electronic carnival.
  • Dualist Inquiry: Driving the indie-electronic scene with brilliant guitar-synth loops.

The International Icons (10)

  • Ed Sheeran: Taking the city by storm in 2025 on his Mathematics tour, playing "Castle on the Hill" to an absolute sea of fans.
  • Megadeth: Setting the gold standard for heavy metal in the Northeast at NH7 Weekender.
  • Lucas: The K-pop superstar who brought unprecedented hysteria to the Cherry Blossom Festival.
  • Akon: Packing the festival grounds with classic R&B stadium energy.
  • Boney M: Bringing their historic farewell disco tour straight to Meghalaya.
  • Eric Martin (Mr. Big): Cementing the city's love affair with classic 80s/90s rock.
  • Scorpions: Hard rock legends delivering an unforgettable night of soaring guitar solos.
  • Ne-Yo: Providing the perfect smooth R&B soundtrack under the November skies.
  • Bryan Adams: A monumental classic act that proved Shillong's pulling power early on.
  • Firehouse: The kings of the rock ballad, deeply revered by local guitarists.
